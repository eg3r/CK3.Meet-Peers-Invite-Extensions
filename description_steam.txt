[h1]Meet Peers - Invite Extensions[/h1]

[b]Let your children meet children beyond your own realm, and make friends, learn and grow with them.[/b]

In the base game, Meet Peers only brings together children from your own realm and your closest relations, Recreation is its only goal, and only children who hold a title can host one. This mod opens Meet Peers up: more children to meet, a goal for your child, AI children who host and invite you, and Meet Peers hosted by the children at your court.

[h2]More Children to Meet[/h2]
New guest options in the Meet Peers planner:
[list]
[*] [b]Neighboring Children[/b] - The children of the realms next door, including across the water. On by default.
[*] [b]Children of Court and Vassals[/b] - When you plan a Meet Peers for a child at your court: the other children at your court and your vassals' children. On by default.
[*] [b]Children of the Same Faith[/b] - Children of your faith from courts further away. Tick it when you want it.
[*] [b]Nearby Player Children[/b] - In multiplayer, the other players' children who live nearby. On by default.
[/list]
[b]Family First[/b] - The host's brothers, sisters and cousins are invited before anyone else, and they are more likely to come.

[h2]A Goal for Your Child[/h2]
When you plan a Meet Peers or join one, pick a goal and the child it is aimed at:
[list]
[*] [b]Befriend a Peer[/b] - Try to win a new friend. A gift, your standing, or your child's own charm can help.
[*] [b]Learn from a Peer[/b] - Learn from a child who is better than yours at something.
[*] [b]Role Model[/b] - Look up to another child and take after one of their personality traits.
[/list]
Once both children have arrived, a moment together comes up during the Meet Peers, with choices that suit your child's nature. If it isn't the right moment, your child can do something else instead: play with the others, slip off to the chapel, find a quiet corner or show off. Everything that happens is noted in the activity log.

[h2]Your Children Host at Court[/h2]
A king's children usually live at court without a title of their own, so in the base game they never host a Meet Peers. Now you plan it for them, and your child is the host.
[list]
[*] [b]Plan It Yourself[/b] - Pick the Young Host in the planner. You pay for it, your child hosts it at your capital, and you stay home.
[*] [b]A Child Asks[/b] - Now and then a child at court asks for a Meet Peers. Say yes, and the planner opens with that child already chosen; a reminder at the top of the screen takes you back to it later. Say no, and your child is disappointed in you.
[*] [b]How It Went[/b] - When it's over, you hear how it went: how many children came, who of your family was there, and whom your child made friends with.
[*] [b]Grateful Children[/b] - The young host thinks better of you, and so does every child of your family who comes.
[*] [b]AI Courts Too[/b] - AI kings and emperors plan Meet Peers for their children as well.
[/list]

[h2]A Livelier World[/h2]
[list]
[*] [b]AI Children Host More[/b] - AI child rulers host Meet Peers far more often, spread out over their childhood.
[*] [b]You Get Invited[/b] - AI children who live near your child put your child on their guest list, and children of your faith invite yours from further away.
[*] [b]Not All in One Place[/b] - Meet Peers spread out across the map instead of crowding one area, so the children around aren't split between too many at the same time.
[*] [b]At War? Still Welcome[/b] - Your child can still go to a Meet Peers while your realm is at war.
[/list]

[h2]Cooldown[/h2]
[list]
[*] [b]Host More Often[/b] - Your child can host a Meet Peers every 2 years instead of every 3.
[*] [b]Reset the Cooldown[/b] - An optional decision to plan the next Meet Peers right away. It's off unless you switch it on, and is meant as a cheat.
[/list]

[h2]Game Rules[/h2]
Everything can be tuned when you start a game: pick [b]Meet Peers[/b] in the game rule filter to see only these rules (they're also listed under Tweaks).
[list]
[*] [b]Meet Peers Cooldown[/b] - 1, 2 (default) or 3 years.
[*] [b]Meet Peers Cooldown Reset[/b] - Off (default) or On.
[*] [b]AI Meet Peers and Player Children[/b] - Off (base game), Nearby, or Nearby and Same Faith (default).
[*] [b]Meet Peers Guests at War[/b] - Allowed for players (default) or Not Allowed.
[*] [b]Court Children Host Meet Peers[/b] - Off, Kings and Above (default), or Dukes and Above.
[/list]

[h2]Languages[/h2]
English, French, German, Spanish, Polish, Russian, Chinese, Korean and Japanese.

[h2]Compatibility[/h2]
This mod changes the Meet Peers activity itself, so it doesn't work together with other mods that change Meet Peers.
