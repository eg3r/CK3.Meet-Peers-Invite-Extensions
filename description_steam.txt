[h1]Meet Peers - Invite Extensions[/h1]

[b]Let your child ruler meet children beyond your own realm, and make friends, learn and grow with them.[/b]

[h2]Overview[/h2]
In vanilla, the Meet Peers activity only offers children from your own realm and your personal relations, and Recreation is its only goal. This mod adds new guest invite options so you can bring in the children next door or children of your faith. It also adds goals aimed at one of the other children: befriend them, learn from them, or take after them.

[h2]Guest Invite Options[/h2]
[list]
[*] [b]Neighboring Children[/b] - Neighboring rulers who are children themselves, and the children of rulers of bordering realms, including across water and the count next door in another duchy. On by default.
[*] [b]Children of the Same Faith[/b] - Children of your faith at any court in diplomatic range. Opt-in.
[*] [b]Realm First[/b] - New options fill after your own realm's children, so they keep priority
[/list]

[h2]Meet Peers Goals[/h2]
Pick a goal and the child it is aimed at when planning Meet Peers, or later during the activity. Guests can pick one too.
[list]
[*] [b]Befriend a Peer[/b] - A chance to become friends (10-90%). It is better if you are already potential friends, if they like you, and if your diplomacy is high. Bring a gift, use your standing (prestige, or influence in administrative realms), or use your Charming, Rowdy or Curious nature, for a better chance.
[*] [b]Learn from a Peer[/b] - A chance to gain +1 in the skill where they are furthest ahead of you, or in their second-best skill at a lower chance, prowess included (15-70%). It is better with a bigger gap and higher learning. Spend piety or standing for a better chance.
[*] [b]Role Model[/b] - A chance to take on one of their personality traits, or another of their traits at a lower chance (20-40%). It is better if you are friends or close in age. Spend piety for a better chance. You see the trait first.
[*] [b]A Moment Together[/b] - Once you have both arrived, a moment usually comes up (about 80%) at a random point during the Meet Peers, with a second, smaller chance (about 30%) in the last month if it doesn't. If you try and fail, you may get one more try a few weeks later. Every event also offers an alternative pastime with a small sure reward (less stress, piety or prestige). Results are recorded in the activity log, and AI children keep Recreation.
[/list]

[h2]Cooldown[/h2]
[list]
[*] [b]Shorter Cooldown[/b] - Meet Peers can be hosted every 2 years instead of every 3. The "Meet Peers Cooldown" game rule sets it to 1, 2 or 3 years.
[*] [b]Reset Meet Peers Cooldown[/b] - A decision that lets you plan the next Meet Peers right away instead of waiting for the cooldown. Off by default: enable it with the "Meet Peers Cooldown Reset" game rule. This is considered cheating and should not be active for normal gameplay.
[/list]

[h2]AI Children Host and Invite You[/h2]
[list]
[*] [b]More AI Meet Peers[/b] - AI child rulers host about once per childhood as counts, up to four times as emperors, spread out over time.
[*] [b]You Get Invited[/b] - AI children nearby (same or neighboring realm, or close by) put your child at the top of their guest list. Children of your faith also invite you from further away.
[*] [b]More Hosting Near You[/b] - AI children near a player child host more readily, even child counts.
[*] [b]Game Rules[/b] - "AI Meet Peers and Player Children" (Off / Nearby / Nearby and Same Faith; Off restores base-game AI hosting), and "Meet Peers Guests at War", which lets player children at war attend (allowed by default).
[/list]

[h2]Languages[/h2]
English, French, German, Spanish, Polish, Russian, Chinese, Korean, and Japanese

[h2]Requirements[/h2]
[list]
[*] Vanilla Meet Peers rules still apply: guests must be children aged 4-13, healthy, free, not hostages, and within diplomatic range
[*] Invite options add guests together: a child is invited if any ticked option includes them
[/list]

[h2]Compatibility[/h2]
This mod replaces the vanilla Meet Peers activity file (playdate.txt). It is not compatible with other mods that change the Meet Peers activity.
